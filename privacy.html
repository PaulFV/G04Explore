2. What G04Explore stores

G04Explore is a personal place-saving app. Places, lists, notes, ratings, visit status, your name, theme, language and notification/offline preferences are stored locally in your browser or installed app on your device. This data is not uploaded to a G04Explore server by this static app.

Deleting browser/app storage removes locally stored data. The app does not require an account or password.

3. Hosting

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you open the app, GitHub processes your IP address and technical request data (such as time, requested file and browser type) and, according to GitHub, logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. This is necessary to deliver the app securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be processed in the USA. For details, see GitHub's privacy statement.

4. Google Maps Platform and Google Places

The Google Maps script is loaded only when you use the place search. Your search text is then sent to Google Maps Platform (Places API), and the returned place data, map, photos and ratings are displayed. When loading the script, Google also receives your IP address. Google processes these requests under its own terms and privacy policy. The app uses a centrally configured, browser-restricted Google Maps key; the key is not a personal user credential.

Your device location is requested only when you choose “Explore nearby” or an equivalent location action. The app first explains what happens, then the browser asks for permission. If you decline, search remains available without your location. G04Explore does not store your precise location in its local place records.

5. Offline mode, fonts and external links

When enabled, the service worker caches the app shell on your device so that saved local data can be viewed offline. The fonts are hosted with the app itself; loading them does not connect to Google or any other third party. Links to Google Maps and third-party websites open outside the app and are governed by those providers' privacy notices.

6. Notifications

Notifications are optional and use the operating system/browser permission. G04Explore does not send notification content to a G04Explore server. You can revoke permission in the device or browser settings.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. For locally stored app data, you can also clear the app/browser storage. Contact the controller above for privacy requests. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data-protection supervisory authority.
